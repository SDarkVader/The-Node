Every cell is one real plot from seed 7, day 220 — 62 buildings, 38 held, 7 backstopped, 1 vacant. Nothing here is invented for the mockup, so what you pick is what the terminal actually renders.
The problem each one has to solve
The two signals driving colour barely move. District tension sits at 0.08; economic heat spans 0 – 0.5 and is zero for every support role while the district is healthy. On a naive 0–1 ramp the whole node reads flat and dead.
Toggle auto-ranged against raw 0–1 on any board to see the difference. Auto-ranging is the fix; the tradeoff is that a genuinely calm node stops looking calm.
What the map already tells you
Millers are the only consistently hot points on the board. That falls out of the economics rather than the palette — Miller is the role the design keeps deliberately starved, and it turns out to be the thing your eye lands on first without anyone deciding it should.
