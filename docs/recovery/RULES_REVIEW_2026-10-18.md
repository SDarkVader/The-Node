## Rules that change the interpretation of the experiment

1. Information and social exposure are the game; economic stability is the board it runs on. Wealth is not a universal currency or proof of winning. Reputation, cooperation, witnessed deeds, opportunities and bounded consequences cannot be reduced to a single wealth increase. See original brief §0; [containment](../ADVERSARIAL_CONTAINMENT.md), “The economy is the board; reputation is the prize”; [08-11 addendum](../DESIGN_ADDENDUM_2026-08-11.md) §§4–6.
2. Essential output, backstops, Oracle and other mechanical world processes remain non-agentic. An experimental controller supplies only authorized discretionary actions for one existing person. No synthetic-driver or evaluator authority belongs in participant input. Roles follow actual placement, conscription, churn and departure rather than model selection.
3. Personal memory is mortal. Civic records are a different category; infrastructure diagnostics are not actor memory. Do not feed saved prompts, raw replay tapes, private evaluator state or old diary originals into later turns to give perfect recall. Recognition is directional and earned, and cannot be fabricated by annotating another person.
> **Superseded 2026-10-20 (owner decision; see [A01 diary contract](../research/DIARY_CONTRACT_A01.md)):** the author composes one daily entry from the menus and may record faithfully, manipulate or suppress; the game does not generate entries. Principle 4 below is kept as history.

4. The current user's diary clarification is explicit: the game creates/maintains the content and connections; players may mark events but cannot alter those contents. Marking does not grant permission to author arbitrary observations, interpretations or relationships, preserve them indefinitely, or restore expired memory. The [later visual foundation](../DESIGN_NODE_VISUAL_FOUNDATION_2026-08-19.md) §5 explicitly describes the diary as a decaying connection map rather than just written entries. The [housing design](../DESIGN_HOUSING_REPUTATION_2026-08-13.md) §7 introduces its mechanical connections view and trespass risk. Earlier composed-entry wording must not override this clarification.
5. Daily memory degradation is server-owned. Existing tuning is a two-day rolling TTL with independent 0.2 daily drift rolls for observation/interpretation; subject identity and context do not drift. Owner reads must not add another decay roll or extend expiry. The housing design's corrected §7.4 explicitly rejects an extra trespass-read distortion layer. The later visual brief also contains “every single read” wording; it conflicts with that corrected daily-store rule and its own “redrawn daily” wording. No new per-read distortion rule was silently implemented.
6. Breaking in carries a narrowly scoped information reward and a real cost. Under housing §7.3, a trespasser sees the surviving connections/SUBJECT view, not the owner's observations, interpretations, context, full transcript or immortal dossier. Ordinary visitors get no such permission. The owner must be absent, an appropriate crafted key must exist, and witness/detection/consequence mechanics must be authoritative. Neither possessing an owner ID nor a caller-supplied absence boolean is sufficient gameplay authority.
7. Crime requires actual shared-resource preparation, ownership, target, consumption, absence and consequence machinery. Eligibility helpers are not complete acts. Resource and fine flows need real conservation; grifter survival and neutral recovery paths remain available.
8. Do not confuse old opportunity progress with a general reputation system. The [recovered first-inhabitant decisions](EXPERIMENT_FIRST_INHABITANT.md) explicitly describe a later signed/hidden deed-score proposal superseding older “standing never decreases” language, while retaining baseline participation. That proposed general score and monuments are unimplemented; the existing `reputationProgress` scalar is not that system. This review does not rewrite either into a functioning scoring mechanic.

## Correction to the reported two-turn result

Both archived requests were inspected directly. Their authorized inputs had **zero recognition entries, zero diary entries, zero rumours and zero proximity messages**. In both exact provider schemas, `diary` was **`{"type":"null"}`** because there were no earned diary subjects. The model could not submit the old diary action at all. Saying it “chose not to write a diary entry” was incorrect.

The turn-2 move back to the starting plot is an observed reversal. It is neither proof of a policy defect nor evidence of a useful strategy. The wealth increase was a kernel result, not a demonstrated causal benefit of movement. The two turns established transport, bounded accounting, authenticated action admission, replay and restart. They did not establish full compliance with the intended diary/trespass rules or meaningful social play.

## Ordered acceptance work before another social-game experiment

1. **Version the diary/event contract.** A genuine observable interaction must create the correct mechanical record even when the model submits no diary action. Fabricated, unobserved, cross-owner or unknown-identity event marks must fail. Marking twice must not duplicate the event, change its original content or refresh TTL. Retain legacy-tape replay semantics rather than rewriting old actions.
2. **Verify memory and disclosure.** Test day-0 creation, day-1 deterministic drift and day-2 expiry; repeated observation/restart must not produce additional drift. Expired originals and hidden distortion flags never reach policy input. Actor input contains only its current legitimate view; saved diagnostics cannot become perfect recall. Keep identity/context reliability and isolate memory RNG from the economic trajectory.
3. **Complete the abode and restricted-read transaction in prerequisite order.** Bind homes to real people/buildings, resolve absence from authoritative state, then add the crafted-key/witness/consequence path. Test owner present, owner away, owner offline, ordinary visitor, missing/wrong/used key, repeated reveal, caught attempt and resource conservation. A successful trespass reveals only the allowed current connections snapshot and never edits or refreshes the owner's diary.
4. **Exercise a meaningful offline social scenario.** Introduce only legitimate test interactions, not hidden prompt hints or artificial rewards. Confirm automatic memory, earned recognition, marks, expiry and privacy across host and ledger restarts, with complete-state replay. Keep mechanical backstops and basic participation intact.
5. **Resume a bounded live decision only after its actual action/observation contract is verified.** Use the same authorized model and budget, distinguish available from unavailable actions, and report real effects and limitations. No new provider request or allowance reset was needed for this review.

The smallest first component is the grounded, versioned event-to-diary/marking contract. Implementing break-in access before that would expose the wrong memory representation.
