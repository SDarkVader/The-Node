This is a recording, not a game — you can watch the town, you cannot walk it.

What happened

A recorded run of NODE's engine — the same code the tests and simulations use, stepped one day at a time with synthetic stand-ins talking on the Wall. Nothing here is mocked up. What it is not: a game. There is no avatar, nobody moves, and roughly a third of the people — those without a role — have no position on the map at all. Being inside the place is the Godot client's job.
