# Competitive reputation society: work in progress

The intended experiment gives every inhabitant the same primary objective: gain community reputation through daily activity and interactions. Every inhabitant can use the same available means to influence others. One additional model-controlled participant starts as a roleless grifter, alongside the two Bakers and Miller. Decisions and outcomes are not scripted.

The offline opt-in `--competitive-grifter` selection adds that fourth controlled person and common instructions. It preserves their persistent person identity when their role changes. The plan accounts for a fourth act decision per turn and the tape records the option for replay. Paid launcher preparation and execution reject this unfinished option.

The PR #60 reducer, copied unchanged from head `b6616c42cd7f857753134eaf968fc0b5b1d696a6`, now consumes public verified paid bread sales, paid bread purchases and actual flour supply in the opt-in society: one positive unit per person, type and day. These three types follow the earlier recorded achievement comparison. They use kernel dealing identities and actual receipts, never rumours or speech. Score history is bound to the replay hash, and a person's own score and scores of currently visible or recognized people appear in their authorized view. This is an experimental mapping to the prototype, not a calibrated or fully validated policy. No negative event source is implemented; see [proposals for review](REPUTATION_LOSS_PROPOSAL_2026-10-30.md).

Legacy opportunity progress remains distinct and comes from shift cover. Vacancies and assignment follow existing churn, fill and conscription; existing performance preference operates when another-role conscription occurs. In the daily routine a turn-limit stop does not complete the day and is not proof of negligence. No automatic inactivity eviction or fabricated takeover is introduced.
