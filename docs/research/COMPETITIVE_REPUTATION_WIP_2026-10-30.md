# Competitive reputation society: work in progress

The intended experiment gives every inhabitant the same primary objective: gain community reputation through daily activity and interactions. Every inhabitant can use the same available means to influence others. One additional model-controlled participant starts as a roleless grifter, alongside the two Bakers and Miller. Decisions and outcomes are not scripted.

The offline opt-in `--competitive-grifter` selection adds that fourth controlled person and common instructions. It preserves their persistent person identity when their role changes. The plan accounts for a fourth act decision per turn and the tape records the option for replay. Paid launcher preparation and execution reject this unfinished option.

The PR #60 reducer, copied unchanged from head `b6616c42cd7f857753134eaf968fc0b5b1d696a6`, consumes verified paid bread sales, paid bread purchases and actual flour supply: one positive unit per person, type and day. A completed day that the occupant explicitly finishes without work adds one negative unit. Interrupted runs do not settle a day or incur a penalty. An explicit trial baseline, equal to one positive unit in the model, gives every person positive starting standing without inventing a deed or notoriety. The event weight and baseline are trial settings; there is no preselected duration for inactivity. Scores and events are replay-bound; authorized views expose one's own score and scores of visible or recognized people. This experimental mapping is not calibrated.

Legacy opportunity progress remains distinct and comes from shift cover. When a role holder's standing crosses to zero or below, an intervention begins for the business; recovery above zero within three subsequent completed days ends it, otherwise the occupant becomes roleless and the business becomes backstopped. Known vacant or backstopped roles may be claimed by any inhabitant, with valid requests resolved in submission order. A turn-limit stop does not complete the day and is not evidence of negligence. Mechanical support during intervention currently produces anonymous output for Miller and Import/Export; equivalent Baker, Courier and Investigator support is not implemented. This is not evidence of model behaviour or social influence.

Further work: implement and verify support for the other business roles without inventing market prices, supply or witness effects; validate the complete influence-to-replacement flow and hosted checks.
