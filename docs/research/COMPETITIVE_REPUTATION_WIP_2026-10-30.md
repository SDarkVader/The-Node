# Competitive reputation society: work in progress

The intended experiment gives every inhabitant the same primary objective: gain community reputation through daily activity and interactions. Every inhabitant can use the same available means to influence others. One additional model-controlled participant starts as a roleless grifter, alongside the two Bakers and Miller. Decisions and outcomes are not scripted.

The offline opt-in `--competitive-grifter` selection adds that fourth controlled person and common instructions. It preserves their persistent person identity when their role changes. The plan accounts for a fourth act decision per turn and the tape records the option for replay. Paid launcher preparation and execution reject this unfinished option. This is only a controller foundation: it does not implement the requested reputation system or demonstrate a takeover.

In the current kernel, legacy opportunity progress comes from shift cover; social speech does not directly earn reputation. Vacancies and assignment are governed by existing churn, fill and conscription mechanics. In the daily routine an inhabitant who does not complete required duties can cause the turn limit to stop the run; a null decision does not automatically generate a backstopped vacancy. The scoring kernel and its test are copied unchanged from draft PR #60 head `b6616c42cd7f857753134eaf968fc0b5b1d696a6` as the intended starting point; it assumes authenticated events and is not yet integrated. Do not treat these paths as proof of loss or gain of social reputation, manipulation, or a takeover.

Further work before treating this as the requested experiment: define authoritative evidence and persistent reputation gains and losses from real daily events; expose the relevant facts to each participant without leaking private state; implement and test a fair, non-scripted route from sustained occupational failure to a vacancy and eligible replacement, preserving survival and recovery; validate replay, historical compatibility, model request binding and accounting; review hosted CI. The objective text must be revisited against the actual mechanics. No live model run or expenditure was performed.
